About

Creating Memorable Experiences Since 2002

I enjoy creating positive memorable experiences for people. Through 25 years of developing, customizing, and managing brand campaigns, the key learning that stands out is that everything is relationship oriented.

Saying I've been involved with over 5,000 events and distributed over a million product samples is impressive quantitatively — but the qualitative aspects of brand building and networking establish long-term growth.

"It's the combination of passion, drive, and mentality in doing things the right way, having the right people involved, and making things happen that leave people with the WOW factor."

I bring to the table an established network of resources and assets to solve your brand marketing requirements — whether that's staffing, events, production, tours, or full-service campaign management.
